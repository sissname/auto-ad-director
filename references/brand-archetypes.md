# Brand and Segment Archetypes

Use this reference to select a visual direction before writing automotive campaign prompts. Treat named brands as inspiration for visual language, not as factual claims about current campaigns or product specifications.

## Selection Rule

If the user supplies only a vehicle name, infer the closest archetype and state it briefly. If the user supplies a brand tone, use that over the archetype.

```text
Archetype: [one name]
Why: [vehicle tier, audience, desired emotion]
Visual language: [location + light + lens + color + material]
Avoid: [style that would break the brand tone]
```

## Archetypes

### Tech-Luxury EV

- Use for: premium electric sedans, smart EV launches, urban executive campaigns.
- Mood: precise, intelligent, quiet power.
- Locations: glass towers, clean overpasses, rooftop plazas, modern galleries, rain-slick financial districts.
- Light: blue-hour rain, hard architectural sunlight, controlled showroom diffusion.
- Palette: silver, graphite, deep blue, cold white, small warm city accents.
- Avoid: loud racing aggression, messy street clutter, heavy neon cyberpunk.

### Performance Sports

- Use for: coupe, performance sedan, track-inspired trims, launch acceleration visuals.
- Mood: tension, speed, grip, controlled violence.
- Locations: tunnel exits, mountain hairpins, empty industrial roads, night gas stations, concrete ramps.
- Light: low raking light, headlight beams, motion streaks, sharp shadows.
- Palette: black, red, asphalt gray, sodium vapor, brake-light accents.
- Avoid: static catalog poses, soft lifestyle smiles, too many props.

### Executive Luxury

- Use for: flagship sedans, chauffeur contexts, premium business audiences.
- Mood: authority, silence, status, privacy.
- Locations: hotel portico, art museum, embassy street, quiet CBD arrival, polished stone driveway.
- Light: soft dusk, warm interior spill, black-car reflections, rain gloss.
- Palette: black, champagne, marble, warm gray, deep green or burgundy accents.
- Avoid: flashy lens flare, teenage streetwear energy, chaotic crowds.

### Youth Smart Mobility

- Use for: compact EVs, city commuters, creator-focused launches.
- Mood: clever, agile, optimistic, design-forward.
- Locations: colorful urban blocks, campus roads, cafes, design stores, sunny residential streets.
- Light: crisp daylight, playful reflections, clean shadows.
- Palette: white, pastel accents, sky blue, fresh green, polished plastic/metal.
- Avoid: overly heavy luxury darkness or aggressive performance language.

### Family Premium

- Use for: SUVs, MPVs, family-safe premium vehicles.
- Mood: trust, space, comfort, protection.
- Locations: modern home driveway, coastal road, school-adjacent boulevard, weekend resort, warm garage.
- Light: golden morning, soft cabin light, clear readable interiors.
- Palette: pearl white, warm gray, tan leather, natural green, soft sky.
- Avoid: dangerous speed cues, cold isolation, fashion poses that ignore comfort.

### Off-Road Adventure

- Use for: SUVs, pickups, rugged lifestyle campaigns.
- Mood: capability, terrain, independence.
- Locations: desert plain, forest trail, snow road, cliff overlook, muddy track.
- Light: sunrise/sunset side light, dust beams, storm edge light.
- Palette: sand, olive, basalt, snow white, amber highlights.
- Avoid: mall-crawler polish unless the user requests urban off-road fashion.

### China New-Luxury Launch

- Use for: Chinese EV launch visuals, social-first premium campaigns, city landmark imagery.
- Mood: confident, modern, aspirational, culturally local without tourist cliches.
- Locations: Shanghai wet streets, Shenzhen glass, Chengdu art districts, Hangzhou lakeside tech parks, Chongqing layered roads.
- Light: blue-hour rain, reflective night, golden skyline, hard sun on glass.
- Palette: silver, ceramic white, ink black, glass blue, one brand accent.
- Avoid: generic "future city" clutter, overdone neon, random Chinese text inside the image.

## Tone Levers

Use 2-3 levers per campaign:

- Premium: restrained palette, clean surfaces, fewer objects, controlled reflections.
- Fashion: wardrobe texture, distant expression, editorial pose, cropped body lines.
- Technology: macro light bars, glass, UI-like clean negative space, cool reflections.
- Performance: low angle, road texture, tire stance, directional light, motion cues.
- Trust: readable cabin, soft light, human scale, calm environment.

## Brand Safety

- Avoid claiming exact official brand identity unless the user provides it.
- For real brands, prefer "inspired by [tone]" over "official campaign".
- Do not ask image models to generate exact badges, slogans, plates, or legal copy. Request clean areas for later design placement.
