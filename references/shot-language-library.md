# Shot Language Library

Use this reference when building shot lists, contact sheets, prompt bibles, or visual systems. Pick shots that serve the brief; do not force every campaign to use every shot type.

## Shot Selection Rule

Every campaign needs a role mix, not a random collection:

```text
Hero: sells the campaign.
Product: proves the car.
Human: creates desire or use context.
Detail: adds craft/technology.
Environment: builds world.
Social: crops cleanly for feed/poster.
```

## Automotive Shot Types

### Low Three-Quarter Front Hero

- Role: primary product desire and stance.
- Composition: car points into frame; wheels grounded; road shadow confirms contact.
- Lens: 28-45mm depending on drama.
- Best for: performance, launch hero, city arrival.
- Prompt cue: "low three-quarter front view, tires firmly touching wet asphalt, strong shoulder line."
- Risk: ultra-wide distortion, broken front geometry.

### Side Profile Architecture

- Role: design clarity and premium order.
- Composition: car as horizontal line against vertical architecture.
- Lens: 35-70mm.
- Best for: Audi-like precision, sedans, executive EVs.
- Prompt cue: "clean side profile, architectural verticals, restrained negative space."
- Risk: static catalog feel; add light signature or reflection.

### Open Door Fashion Diagonal

- Role: human/product relationship.
- Composition: open door creates diagonal; talent sits, leans, or steps out.
- Lens: 35-50mm.
- Best for: fashion editorial, urban arrival, social poster.
- Prompt cue: "open door as metallic diagonal, model separated from vehicle, clean text area."
- Risk: merged limbs and car door; clarify body position.

### Hood Top-Down Portrait

- Role: graphic impact and social crop.
- Composition: hood dominates; talent enters from edge; hair/hand creates line.
- Lens: 28-35mm high angle.
- Best for: fashion-performance, youth tech, striking poster.
- Prompt cue: "high-angle over hood, accurate hood curvature, subject at lower-right third."
- Risk: distorted hood or fused reflection.

### Rear Light / Logo Macro

- Role: technology and craft proof.
- Composition: logo/light bar as jewelry detail.
- Lens: 85-100mm macro.
- Best for: EV lighting, Audi-like precision, premium launch.
- Prompt cue: "illuminated light bar macro, shallow depth, clean emblem area, no random text."
- Risk: gibberish lettering; avoid exact text generation.

### Wheel and Ground Contact Detail

- Role: performance, stance, realism.
- Composition: tire, ground, shadow, brake detail.
- Lens: 70-100mm close detail.
- Best for: sports cars, off-road, premium material proof.
- Prompt cue: "correct wheel alignment, tire sidewall touching asphalt, contact shadow."
- Risk: broken spokes, floating tire.

### Wet Street Reflection Wide

- Role: atmosphere and cinematic world.
- Composition: car mid-ground; wet pavement leads eye to product.
- Lens: 28-35mm.
- Best for: Shanghai/Shenzhen rain, executive arrival, tech luxury.
- Prompt cue: "single real car, reflection on wet asphalt, accurate reflection logic."
- Risk: duplicate vehicles or melted reflections.

### Glass Wall Double Narrative

- Role: premium complexity and art direction.
- Composition: real car/person plus controlled reflection.
- Lens: 35-50mm.
- Best for: luxury EV, editorial campaign, architecture.
- Prompt cue: "real subject plus mirror reflection, no duplicate real person."
- Risk: reflection confusion; state exactly what is reflected.

### Rooftop Architecture Hero

- Role: scale, launch grandeur, future city.
- Composition: car in city-stage environment; skyline/facade frames product.
- Lens: 24-35mm.
- Best for: EV launch, tech-luxury, concept-like campaign.
- Prompt cue: "geometric rooftop plaza, skyline, realistic scale, clean commercial photography."
- Risk: car becomes too small; keep subject hierarchy clear.

### Executive Arrival

- Role: status, privacy, comfort.
- Composition: car near hotel/museum/portico; human gesture minimal.
- Lens: 50-85mm.
- Best for: Mercedes-Benz, NIO ET9, flagship sedans.
- Prompt cue: "quiet executive arrival, warm cabin spill, polished stone, long-lens calm."
- Risk: too passive; add precise light/material detail.

### Family Cabin Comfort

- Role: trust and spaciousness.
- Composition: readable cabin, seats, light, family scale.
- Lens: 24-35mm interior or 35-50mm lifestyle.
- Best for: Li Auto, SUV/MPV, family premium.
- Prompt cue: "spacious rear cabin, warm soft daylight, readable seat geometry, calm family use."
- Risk: cramped cabin, distorted seats, extra limbs.

### Performance Road Motion

- Role: energy and driver desire.
- Composition: road direction and body curve carry motion.
- Lens: 35-70mm tracking feel.
- Best for: Porsche, BMW, sports trims.
- Prompt cue: "controlled motion cue, road grip, low raking light, body geometry visible."
- Risk: excessive blur hiding the product.

## Contact Sheet Recipe

For 12 frames, use:

1. Hero exterior.
2. Alternate crop hero.
3. Human/product interaction.
4. Macro light/logo detail.
5. Wheel/ground detail.
6. Interior/cabin.
7. Wet reflection or mirror shot.
8. Architecture wide.
9. Social poster crop.
10. Rear or side profile.
11. Night/dusk mood.
12. Experimental brand image.

Swap roles based on brand. A family SUV needs more cabin/lifestyle; a sports car needs more motion/stance.

## Constraint Reminder

Shot language is a menu, not a checklist. If the user's brief is narrow, pick 2-4 strong shot types and stop.
