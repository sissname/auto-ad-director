# Examples

Use these examples as compact patterns. Do not copy the vehicle or location unless the user asks.

## Example 1: Luxury EV City Hard-Light Hero

Brief: Create a hero image for a silver luxury EV in a dense city street with hard afternoon light.

Style anchor: Premium electric vehicle campaign + urban architecture + hard editorial sunlight. Core materials are silver metallic paint, dark glass, red brick, and deep blue shadow. The car must feel real, grounded, and expensive, with clean logo areas and restrained contrast.

Midjourney:

```text
cinematic luxury EV campaign hero shot, silver electric sportback parked in a shadowed city canyon, red brick pavement, tall glass and brick buildings, hard late-afternoon sunlight slicing across the hood and roofline, deep blue shadows with retained detail, realistic metallic paint reflections, low camera 35mm lens, premium automotive advertising, grounded wheels, accurate vehicle proportions --ar 16:9 --style raw --stylize 120 --chaos 4 --quality 1 --no extra people, warped car body, floating wheels, broken wheel geometry, plastic paint, melted buildings, random watermark, gibberish logo, overdone HDR, blown sky, crushed black shadows
```

Generic Chinese:

```text
主题：银灰色豪华电动车的城市硬光广告主视觉。画面：车辆停在高楼夹出的城市街谷中，红砖地面，玻璃和砖楼背景。构图：低机位 35mm，车身占据下方主视觉，建筑垂直线形成压迫感。光线：下午硬阳光从侧上方切入，在车顶和引擎盖形成清晰高光，阴影深蓝但保留细节。色彩材质：银灰金属车漆、黑色玻璃、红砖、冷蓝阴影。风格：高级汽车广告、真实摄影、低饱和高对比。负面约束：不要车身变形、漂浮轮胎、错乱车轮、塑料车漆、建筑融化、乱码车标、随机水印、过度 HDR。
```
