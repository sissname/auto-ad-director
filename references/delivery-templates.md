## Shot List Table

Use this for production planning:

```text
| Shot | Role | Frame | Subject | Location | Light | Lens | Platform | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Hero | 16:9 | [vehicle] | [place] | [light] | [lens] | [MJ/NB/etc.] | [risk] |
```

Shot roles:

- Hero: primary campaign image.
- Talent: human/product relationship.
- Detail: logo, light bar, wheel, material.
- Environment: city, road, architecture.
- Interior: cabin, interface, comfort.
- Social: crop-first image for feed/poster.

## Versioned Iteration Log

Use for multi-round image production:

```text
V1:
- Prompt:
- Result:
- Score:
- Issue:

V2:
- Change:
- Reason:
- Expected effect:
- Risk:
```

Keep version notes short. Record only changes that affect output.
