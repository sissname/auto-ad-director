# Prompt Bible 示例

以下示例用于提醒输出要有“统一视觉 DNA + 分 shot 逻辑”，而不是一堆散 prompt。

## Prompt Bible 1：香水 / 雨夜玻璃花香

### Project

- Category：香水
- Direction：白花香气，但不走婚礼花海；冷夜、潮湿、玻璃、肌肤距离

### Style anchor

潮湿夜色中的玻璃香水主视觉，冷深色背景，窄而干净的轮廓高光，液体像被空气照亮而不是被打亮，花意通过空气密度和局部倒影传达，而不是通过堆满花瓣。

### Shot system

- Hero：瓶身站在微湿石面或深色漆面，轻长焦，轮廓明确，留一侧文案位。
- Macro：瓶肩、喷头、液体边界、瓶盖接缝。
- Hand / Body Relation：手持靠近锁骨或颈侧，动作克制，强调距离感。

### Prompt stack

- Hero Prompt：
  cinematic luxury perfume still life, thick glass bottle with precise silhouette, cool wet night atmosphere, restrained white floral emotion translated through humid air and reflections, narrow clean rim light, deep charcoal and blue-black palette, premium editorial still life, realistic liquid transparency, controlled negative space for typography
- Macro Prompt：
  macro perfume bottle detail, focus on glass edge thickness and liquid boundary, narrow specular highlight, refined cap seam, shallow depth of field with controlled focus plane, no fake glitter
- Body relation Prompt：
  luxury perfume near collarbone, graceful hand and neck relation, intimate distance, skin texture natural, bottle proportion realistic, fragrance mood carried by humidity and shadow, not by decorative petals

### Negative base

avoid cheap flower explosion, generic black gold luxury, warped bottle, muddy glass, syrup-like liquid, unreadable label as focus, extra fingers

## Prompt Bible 2：腕表 / 钛金属深蓝夜航

### Project

- Category：腕表
- Direction：精密、冷静、工程感，不要数码科技海报

### Style anchor

高级钟表摄影逻辑下的冷色夜航氛围，重点是钛金属拉丝、深蓝表盘层级和干净反光带，画面像精密仪器而不是炫技 CG。

### Shot system

- Hero：略带角度的单表 hero，保住表壳厚度和表耳转折。
- Macro：表冠、拉丝、刻度、指针轴心。
- Hand / Body Relation：轻微腕部动作，袖口或驾驶动作提供尺度。

### Prompt stack

- Hero Prompt：
  luxury watch hero shot, brushed titanium case, deep blue layered dial, disciplined specular band on sapphire crystal, precise case chamfers, high-end horology photography, cool graphite and midnight blue palette, restrained dramatic light, realistic proportions
- Macro Prompt：
  macro luxury watch detail, titanium brushing, crown geometry, dial texture and applied markers, clean reflection control, shallow but intentional depth of field, no plastic chrome feel
- Body relation Prompt：
  premium watch on wrist, believable wrist angle, subtle motion from real gesture, sleeve texture secondary, watch remains hero, skin and metal both realistic, editorial restraint

### Negative base

avoid warped bracelet links, oversized case, fake dial print, messy reflections, cyberpunk HUD, cheap blue neon, impossible wrist pose

## Prompt Bible 3：珠宝 / 祖母绿肌肤温差

### Project

- Category：珠宝
- Direction：收藏级祖母绿，不要婚庆闪爆，不要廉价火彩

### Style anchor

温润皮肤与冷贵宝石的对照，闪点少而准，金属托架稳定，色彩集中在宝石本身，整体像珠宝杂志近景而不是影楼大片。

### Shot system

- Hero：耳饰或吊坠单独 still life，强调结构与切面。
- Macro：爪镶、切面、边缘金属。
- Hand / Body Relation：耳垂、颈侧、锁骨或手指局部。

### Prompt stack

- Hero Prompt：
  luxury jewelry still life, emerald gemstone with precise cut planes, refined metal setting, dark neutral environment with selective highlights, collectible-grade editorial image, restrained sparkle, tactile premium finish
- Macro Prompt：
  macro emerald jewelry detail, prong setting accuracy, gemstone depth and internal light, polished metal edge, controlled micro highlights, no noisy glitter bursts
- Body relation Prompt：
  emerald jewelry against natural skin, elegant ear or collarbone crop, skin alive and refined, gemstone remains crisp, intimate luxury editorial mood, no bridal studio styling

### Negative base

avoid plastic gemstone, starburst glitter everywhere, impossible prongs, mannequin skin, wedding studio backdrop, overexposed shine

## Prompt Bible 4：包袋 / 都市结构与皮革温度

### Project

- Category：包袋
- Direction：都会通勤高端包袋，结构感强，但不是硬广 catalog

### Style anchor

城市秩序感与手工皮革温度并存，包型稳定，皮纹和五金克制精致，背景像建筑材料和真实光线环境，而不是浮夸秀场道具。

### Shot system

- Hero：包袋完整轮廓，台面或建筑边角 supporting。
- Macro：皮纹、针脚、扣具、链条节奏。
- Hand / Body Relation：手提、臂弯或肩背，动作自然，比例可信。

### Prompt stack

- Hero Prompt：
  luxury handbag hero shot, structured leather silhouette, refined grain and edge paint, premium hardware accents with restrained shine, urban architectural still life, soft directional light, editorial luxury product photography, realistic volume and seams
- Macro Prompt：
  macro handbag craftsmanship detail, leather grain, stitching precision, metal clasp geometry, subtle waxed sheen, controlled falloff, tactile realism
- Body relation Prompt：
  luxury handbag carried by hand or on shoulder, believable weight and grip, garment secondary, bag silhouette protected, metropolitan movement, polished but natural pose

### Negative base

avoid collapsed bag shape, plastic leather, overbright hardware, broken chain links, catalog white background, awkward carrying pose, generic black-gold luxury
