# Campaign Research Bank

Use this reference when the user wants research-backed brand tone, mature campaign direction, or benchmark/audit work. This bank stores source links, observation notes, and prompt-design implications only. Do not download, embed, or reproduce official images.

## Xiaomi

Brand: Xiaomi / SU7 and YU7-style tech-performance EV

Sources:

- https://www.mi.com/global/discover/article?id=6117
- https://www.mi.com/global/discover/article?id=6670
- https://www.mi.com/global/discover/article?id=6996

Visual evidence:

- Official Xiaomi pages and search-visible snippets emphasize SU7/YU7 as Xiaomi EV products, with strong color naming such as Aqua Blue, Verdant Green, and Mineral Gray.
- The visual territory reads like consumer-electronics polish applied to performance mobility: clean surfaces, confident stance, bright product colors, modern city/tech context.
- The strongest prompt cues are glossy paint, clean speed, UI-like negative space, polished glass, and wet asphalt/night-city reflections when a premium mood is requested.

Prompt rules:

- Use "tech-lifestyle performance" rather than old-money luxury.
- Pair vehicle surfaces with phone-like glass reflections, clean city roads, parking decks, studio-bright design spaces, or rain-blue Shanghai/Shenzhen streets.
- Keep typography clean or reserved for post-design; avoid asking the image model to create Xiaomi lettering.

Negative risks:

- Heavy chauffeur mood, fake racetrack aggression, cluttered cyberpunk, random Chinese text, over-complex neon.

Best benchmark use:

- Xiaomi SU7/YU7 city performance campaign, social-first launch visuals, rain-night prompt bible.

## NIO

Brand: NIO / ET9 and premium smart EV territory

Sources:

- https://www.nio.com/et9
- https://www.nio.com/news/nio-launched-the-smart-electric-executive-flagship-et9

Visual evidence:

- NIO presents ET9 as a smart electric executive flagship, with emphasis on executive class, comfort, intelligent systems, and refined technology.
- The brand tone supports calm premium technology, human-centered confidence, quiet authority, and clean architectural spaces.
- ET9 material direction suggests spacious cabin, executive comfort, subtle tech display, and refined exterior stance rather than aggressive sport drama.

Prompt rules:

- Use "executive smart luxury" and "warm premium technology."
- Favor soft dusk, refined glass pavilions, quiet CBD arrival, lounge-like interiors, polished but humane spaces.
- For human scenes, use composed professionals or relaxed owners; avoid cold isolation unless the brief asks for a distant editorial mood.

Negative risks:

- Racing cues, overly cold sci-fi, messy street grit, teenage fashion energy, generic luxury without smart-tech warmth.

Best benchmark use:

- NIO ET9 executive luxury prompt bible, family/professional premium dusk shots, interior comfort support shots.

## Li Auto

Brand: Li Auto / L9 family flagship SUV

Sources:

- https://www.liauto.com/L9
- https://ir.lixiang.com/news-releases/news-release-details/li-auto-inc-unveils-li-l9-its-flagship-smart-suv

Visual evidence:

- Li Auto describes Li L9 as a flagship smart SUV and a six-seat flagship family SUV.
- Official launch positioning emphasizes family users, flagship scale, comfort, intelligence, and SUV practicality.
- The visual world should feel warm, safe, spacious, and premium rather than fashion-cold or speed-focused.

Prompt rules:

- Use "family flagship comfort" as the core phrase.
- Prioritize readable cabin space, morning/golden-hour warmth, home/resort/weekend contexts, calm parents, safe road staging.
- For exterior prompts, show confident SUV stance and generous space without making the vehicle look aggressive or fragile.

Negative risks:

- Dangerous speed cues, lonely fashion poses, dark thriller mood, cramped interiors, racing language, over-abstract luxury.

Best benchmark use:

- Family SUV prompt bible, cabin readability test, premium comfort hero and interior support shots.

## Audi

Brand: Audi / progressive precision and light design

Sources:

- https://www.audi-mediacenter.com/en/photos
- https://www.audi.com/en/innovation/product-innovation/technologies/a6-premium-lighting-design/

Visual evidence:

- Audi's public media resources foreground polished official images, technical precision, and product-focused visual surfaces.
- Audi's A6 e-tron lighting story emphasizes premium lighting design, illuminated rings, digital OLED lighting, and progressive technology.
- The visual language benefits from architectural order, silver/black surfaces, crisp light signatures, cool reflections, and disciplined composition.

Prompt rules:

- Use "progressive precision, premium lighting, architectural order."
- Build scenes around glass architecture, urban plazas, controlled hard shadows, light bars, and metallic paint.
- Macro detail shots should treat lighting elements as technology jewelry.

Negative risks:

- Messy neon, fake logos, over-emotional human acting, cartoon futurism, melted architecture, light effects that obscure vehicle geometry.

Best benchmark use:

- Lighting-detail macro prompts, architectural EV hero shots, cold-tech luxury contact sheets.

## Porsche

Brand: Porsche / 911 performance and sculpted desire

Sources:

- https://newsroom.porsche.com/en.html
- https://press.porsche.com/prod/presse_pag/PressResources.nsf/jumppage/pcna-images?OpenDocument=&hl=pcna-images

Visual evidence:

- Porsche's newsroom and press imagery territory is performance-led, product-desire driven, and strongly tied to stance, road texture, body curves, and driving emotion.
- Porsche-like prompts should protect silhouette and driving context more than generic luxury surfaces.
- Mountain roads, asphalt, low raking light, warm garage/night city, and track-adjacent environments suit the brand better than sterile EV showrooms.

Prompt rules:

- Use "sculpted motion, driver desire, timeless performance."
- Emphasize curve, stance, grip, low light across bodywork, road contact, brake-light accents, and controlled motion cues.
- Avoid exact badge generation; ask for clean emblem area when needed.

Negative risks:

- Fake badges, generic red sports car cliche, floating wheels, sterile glass-office future mood, excessive motion blur that hides body geometry.

Best benchmark use:

- Porsche-like conversion task, mountain performance prompt, no-fake-logo platform adaptation.

## Mercedes-Benz

Brand: Mercedes-Benz / S-Class executive luxury

Sources:

- https://media.mbusa.com/releases/release-cfaf7728c8957661f23433449e0733e4-the-new-mercedes-benz-s-class-refined-in-every-detail

Prompt rules:

- Use "refined executive luxury, comfort, status, silence."

- Human tone should be elegant and mature, not street-fashion aggressive.

Negative risks:

- Street-race energy, noisy youth styling, harsh neon, random grille/badge text, cheap studio shine, cramped cabin.

Best benchmark use:

- S-Class client proposal, executive sedan prompt bible, comfort/status shot list.

## Research Use Rules

- Treat sources as inspiration for prompt direction, not as permission to copy official campaigns.
- If a user asks for exact brand compliance, request official brand guidelines or work from provided material.
- Prefer "brand-inspired visual territory" over "official campaign style."
- Convert research into controllable prompt variables: location family, light signature, material contrast, lens strategy, human tone, and negative risks.
